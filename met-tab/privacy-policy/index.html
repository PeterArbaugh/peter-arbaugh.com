Privacy Policy

Summary

This policy covers the Chrome extension Met Tab and this website. Although I’m not currently collecting any data except for viewing information for this site, every effort will be made to protect privacy.

(Ironically, I wouldn’t have created this page on a site that collects information if Google hadn’t asked for a privacy policy.)

I’m too lazy to set up Google Analytics on the extension, so I’m not currently collecting any usage information. I may implement something as I expand features to watch for errors and streamline features. This policy will be updated if/when that happens.

This website has Google Analytics and collects anonymized usage data which. The data is automatically deleted every 6 months.
